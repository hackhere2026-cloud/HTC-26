    06 / Transmission log

    Questions,
    answered.

    No matching questions. Try “team”, “cost”, or “bring”.

    Who can participate?+

    Students, designers, engineers, researchers and first-time builders aged 18+. Curiosity matters more than credentials.

    Can I come without a team?+

    Absolutely. We run a team-matching session before kickoff, and plenty of strong teams form in the room.

    What should I bring?+

    Your laptop, charger, ID, water bottle and anything special your prototype needs. We provide Wi-Fi, food and basic hardware kits.

    Does it cost anything?+

    No. Hack the Cloud is free for accepted builders. Your pass includes the full event, meals, snacks and a limited-edition kit.
